Why Suneo?

Everyone only remembers Suneo as the rich kid who loves showing off. But if you actually pay attention throughout Doraemon, he's one of the most talented and surprisingly wholesome characters.

Why Suneo is a Good Character?

Maybe, you know this...

  • Suneo is rich.
  • He's Gian's best friend.
  • He's pretty smart.
  • He's nice to animals.

But did you realize...

  • He has many talents.
  • He's socially intelligent.
  • He dreams of becoming a designer.
  • He can cook, sing, draw and write.
  • He's unintentionally hilarious.
  • He truly respects his mother.
